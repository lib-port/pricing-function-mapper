# Regulatory and product limitations

Pricing Function Mapper is technical tooling, not an actuarial opinion,
insurance product, filing, compliance certification, or automated
underwriting authorization.

Before real use, accountable specialists must assess at least:

- jurisdiction-specific rating-factor restrictions and rate-filing duties;
- discrimination, proxy discrimination, fairness, accessibility, and consumer
  impact;
- actuarial credibility, rate adequacy, stability, and adverse selection;
- privacy, data minimization, consent, retention, residency, and security;
- explanations, notices, appeals, human oversight, and record keeping;
- provider/model version change control, monitoring, drift, rollback, and
  incident response;
- whether the configured domain represents the insured population and whether
  audit slices are sufficient.

The synthetic provider and its breakpoints are demonstrations only. A
surrogate can reproduce undesirable or unlawful behavior from its source.
Conformal coverage is a statistical property on the sampled audit
distribution; it is not a guarantee for an individual policyholder or shifted
population.

Do not use predictions outside the saved domain. Do not suppress audit
warnings or treat a passing numerical gate as regulatory approval.
